SayWhat? preview installation

SayWhat? is an unofficial VRChat companion, not made or endorsed by VRChat or
the projects providing its translation components.

The installer uses SayWhat? styling and offers English, Simplified
Chinese, Japanese and Korean. English is initially selected. The selected language
is the fresh app's display-language default, not a translation target. Existing
saved display preferences are kept on ordinary launches. You can change display
language in the app. The software license screen keeps the English original.

The finish-page Open SayWhat? choice is optional and checked by default. It opens the
dashboard with translation stopped. Silent setup does not launch the app.
Updates and removal can safely ask a compatible open app to quit, even from the
tray. If it cannot finish closing, quit from the tray and retry. Setup never
force-kills the app's tools or unloads an unrelated application's models.

Setup still shows measured progress when available; elapsed time is not a
countdown. First speech installation can take a while and reuses valid downloads.
No model, voice capture or translation starts during the update itself.

GET STARTED

Setup defaults to installing Just me. You can choose All users instead; Windows
will ask for administrator permission for shared app files. Each Windows user
keeps separate settings and models. Optional data changes can affect only the
verified initiating account, including all-users installation. Other accounts
keep their data. Unknown identity or alternate-account elevation without the
original token disables cleanup; start setup normally from your own account.

For a registered copy, setup shows the installed version and path, and offers
Update or Repair, or Uninstall. Uninstall removes the registered app without
installing a replacement. A copy in the other installation scope must be managed
or removed first. Unregistered app files are reported but not silently taken over.
Installing an older version requires a separate confirmation.

Your data is kept by default. Verified-account installation/removal offers three
separate unchecked choices: reset preferences, remove saved speakers and log
history, and delete downloaded model files in the app cache. Review the summary
and final confirmation. Imported files outside the app cache and Windows speech
support are kept. Deleted cached files must be downloaded again.
Silent setup/removal keeps data. Cancellation before removal changes nothing.
If app removal or optional cleanup cannot be verified, the app reports a failure;
it does not claim all data was cleared or the app removal was rolled back.

Install the app and open SayWhat from Start. The first-time setup asks for your
spoken language, translation languages and models. Review the download notice, then let the app prepare
its tools. You do not need a separate FoxTrans, .NET or LM Studio/Bionic install,
or a separate manual setup guide for the recommended workflow.

The recommended translator is the smaller Hy-MT2 1.8B model for gaming. A larger
Hy-MT2 7B option is available alongside it on Home and in setup. TranslateGemma
and the English/Japanese-only Liquid translator are optional alternatives with
their own terms. Existing saved model choices are kept.
Model weights are not included in this installer: setup downloads selected
files after consent and verifies pinned checksums.

Windows may ask for administrator approval or a restart. After restarting,
reopen SayWhat? and choose Continue setup; your choices and completed steps
are kept. Neither installation nor finishing setup starts voice capture or
translation. Use Start on Home when you are ready.

New installations use Nemotron streaming recognition directly on Windows with
Vulkan GPU support. Its tools and speech model download during setup; no WSL,
LM Studio or second application is required for the default own-voice workflow.
The previous Voxtral recognizer remains selectable and existing installations
keep it. That optional path still uses the app's managed WSL2 Ubuntu tools and
may require administrator approval, a restart, and additional downloads. The
optional incoming-voice and Groups tools are prepared separately when selected.

Missing Microsoft Visual C++ x64 support is downloaded directly from Microsoft
after consent, checked by hash and Microsoft signature, and installed with
administrator approval. No automatic reboot occurs. An optional unchecked
installer task is also available. The Microsoft package is not redistributed
inside SayWhat?, and its own terms apply.

USE AND CLOSE

The compact dashboard keeps everyday controls together. Detailed audio, model
and troubleshooting options remain under Advanced.

Start your voice translation on Home. Spoken language, output languages, models,
microphone, finishing pause and early translated messages can be changed there.
Timing and early-message changes apply live; language and microphone changes
finish the already captured sentence before changing capture. Model changes may
need file preparation and a brief processing restart, with progress shown.
Up to four output languages share VRChat's short message limit and need more
translation work, so one is the quickest default.

Subtitles for other voices are a separate, optional feature on Home. Close subtitles when
you no longer need them; your own voice translation can continue.
Use Always on top beside Settings in the subtitles window to pin or unpin it.
The choice is remembered for the next time you open subtitles.

On Speakers, choose Enable recognition or Turn off recognition independently of
translation. The voice model loads when suitable clear speech arrives.
Turning it off releases only that model, while translations continue. With recognition
off, the app does not guess who is speaking from their voice or position.
Saved names and voice fingerprints are kept; delete a saved person on Speakers
when you want to remove their confirmed examples.

The first time you press X, choose Quit and stop translation or Keep running in
the tray. You can remember the choice and change it in Settings. Tray mode hides
the main window while translation keeps running; reopen or quit from the icon
beside the clock. Quit stops only SayWhat?'s own services and releases their
model memory. It does not close VRChat or unload another app's models.

PRIVACY AND PREVIEW LIMITS

Speech and translation are processed on your PC. Speech, saved names and voice
fingerprints are not uploaded. Internet access is used for setup downloads, not
cloud translation. Models, preferences, saved people and logs live under
%LOCALAPPDATA%\SayWhat and are kept by default when uninstalling. If you used the former
LocalTalk app, its existing data folder is reused without copying the models.
An existing SayWhat folder takes priority; the two folders are not merged.

The app is called SayWhat?, but Windows files and shortcuts are named SayWhat.
Upgrading keeps the previous installation directory and replaces old app
shortcuts. User preferences, model downloads and speech registrations remain
unless you explicitly select eligible private-data cleanup categories. Windows speech
support and imported models outside the app cache are never removed by these choices.

Speaker learning saves local voice fingerprints, not audio files or transcript
text. Delete a saved person in Speakers to remove their confirmed examples.
Music, detected overlap and unsuitable examples do not teach a person. Recognition can
still be wrong, and live separation of simultaneous speakers is not enabled.
Logs may include spoken or translated text; review them before sharing.

This preview is unsigned and Windows may show a reputation/security warning.
The complete first-run workflow has not been verified on a fresh PC. Real
permission/restart/resume, GPU compatibility and longer live VRChat sessions
still need testing before a wider release. Recovery safeguards reduce known
stalls but do not guarantee accuracy, speed or uninterrupted translation.
