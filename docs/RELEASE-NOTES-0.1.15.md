# SayWhat? 0.1.15 preview

## What’s new

- Translate your voice into up to three languages, on separate lines in one chat bubble. Additional languages are off by default and may increase translation time.
- Download **TranslateGemma 4B** as an alternative translator. It requires choosing the spoken language; Gemma terms apply.
- Download **Liquid AI LFM2 350M EN–JP** for English/Japanese translation only. Other language pairs are unavailable with this model; LFM license terms apply.
- A sidebar connection indicator checks VRChat OSC availability and offers setup guidance. It cannot confirm whether another player saw a message.
- Update notifications on launch, with **Update now** and **Later** choices. Updates verify the download before opening the installer, retain settings and models, and leave Windows permission requests visible.

Hy-MT2 remains the default translator. Existing model selections are preserved, and optional model downloads show their terms before downloading.

## Speaker recognition

Optional local voice recognition can match clear speech to saved people. You can save names and correct matches with isolated voice examples. Names and voice profiles remain on your PC. Turning recognition off releases its model without stopping translation.

Speaker recognition does not separate simultaneous overlapping voices. Music and recordings can still cause wrong matches.

## Preview limitations

This installer is unsigned. Fresh-PC installation, live update handoff, new-model accuracy and speed, and long VRChat sessions still need testing. A larger model is not a guarantee of better or faster translation. VRChat and local models share GPU resources.

Download the Windows installer for normal installation. The complete ZIP, matching **SayWhat-Corresponding-Source.zip**, licenses and checksums accompany this release. GitHub’s automatic “Source code” archives are not the application source.

SayWhat?’s original contributions use GPL-3.0-only; third-party components and models retain their own terms.
